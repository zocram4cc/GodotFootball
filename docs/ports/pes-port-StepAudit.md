## 1. The one cross-cutting defect: the STEP family wrote its speed word to req[0x1c]

`movestep_step.hpp` named `kBandSpeed = 0x1c` ("blended band speed") and used word 0x1d as `kBandSpeedPrev`. Every store in FUN_1406c4c10, FUN_1406c6480, FUN_1406cab20, FUN_1406c6f80, FUN_1406c1070 and FUN_1406d2590 is `param_2[0x1d]` or `param_2[0x22]`; grep of the six decompiles finds no `param_2[0x1c]` (CONFIRMED). The speed ladders (5/11/16 km/h words) therefore never reached the handlers that read word 0x1d (STOP, the turn handlers, the dispatcher's tail). Fixed: `kBandSpeed = 0x1d`, `kBandSpeedCopy = 0x22`, the third alias deleted.
