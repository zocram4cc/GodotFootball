## 1. The writer of `P+0x2818` is found (CONFIRMED)
The touch gate `0x1407EB8A0` reads `P+0x2818` (a byte; `movzx ebx, byte [rdi+0x2818]` at `0x1407EB8D4`) and needs it >= ~162. An image scan of every `[reg+0x2818]` access (capstone over `.text`, `scan` script) gives 13 hits; the writers are:
- `0x1406EB5FF` (`FUN_1406EB440`, the action-entry reset): `mov byte [rbx+0x2818], 0`;
- `0x1406EE769` (`FUN_1406EE6A0`, the reset): `mov word [rdi+0x2818], 0xFFFF` (bytes 0x2818 and 0x2819 = 255);
- **`0x1406F681D` (`FUN_1406F6790`, the per-man anime update, called once per tick by `FUN_1406E5FC0` at `0x1406E60F8`)**: the saturating increment block `0x1406F67C4..0x1406F685F`: bytes `P+0x2814, 2815, 2816, 2817, 2818, 281A, 2819` each `if (b != 0xFF) ++b`, then `P+0x2824` (u16) `if (w != 0) --w`.
So `P+0x2818` is the ticks since the last action change (cleared by the switch, saturating at 255), and the previous patch's hand increment in `RunPesDribbleTicks` was a stand-in at the wrong place (only dribblers, only in the dribble runner). Real captures agree: `live2/pid3332804` `seq045` and `seq048` (the same man, slot 10, consecutive hook samples): `P+0x2814..0x2819` = `FF FF FF FF FF FF`, `P+0x281A` = `0x10` then `0x1A` (counting up), motion word `P+0x2810` = `0xBE5`.
`FUN_1406F6790` itself also calls the default locomotion driver `FUN_1406F0C20` and `FUN_1406F52C0`, which are far outside the translated set, so only its counter block is ported (by hand, statement by statement, citing the addresses above), and it runs once per active man per tick after the action ticks, as `FUN_1406E5FC0` does.
