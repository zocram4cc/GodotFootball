### 3.2 The result and its consumer (CONFIRMED by replay)

- The chain is called as `FUN_1406B4580(anime, 7, ball, &request, out[28])` -> vtable `+0x68` of the Trap action `0x1407999F0` = `jmp 0x1407A01C0`. The call returns `1` (issued) or `0`.

On success `out[0]` is not read; in both issued captures it is 0.
- **Where the clip goes.** Replaying the two issued captures (K1 seq4469 and K5 seq23906) and diffing memory over the call: the **only writes outside the stack are to the Trap action object inside P, `P+0xC80..P+0xCDF` (action `+0x08..+0x64`, 15 write ranges in K1, 13 in K5)**. - So the chain **commits a plan into the action object; it does not issue the motion**. The motion is played later by the action's own tick: Trap vtable `0x1425E1ED0`: `+0x20` = **`FUN_140797CD0(action, P, req)`** (374 lines; calls `FUN_1406B03D0` hit frame, `FUN_1406AF620` duration, `FUN_1406B0180`, the root solver `FUN_1406E85B0`, `FUN_1406F02F0(P, motion, 2)` and `FUN_1406EEBB0(P, 5 or 2)`), `+0x30` = `FUN_1407979F0` (builds the motion request from `P+0x25C8..+0x26D4`), `+0x10` = admission `FUN_14079BBE0`, `+0x70` = `FUN_1407995D0`. Decompile-only: no capture reaches `FUN_140797CD0`.
- After a successful issue the caller (`FUN_1409F8220` TRAP branch, per `pes-port-OwnerPickup2.md` §6) sets `+0x874 = 4`, `+0x878 = 7`, `+0xE6C = 7`. Not re-read this session.
- **What this means for GF:** `playercontroller.cpp:510` calls `PickTrapClip` (rows `0x547..0x57F` of the `0x14348DAC0` row table, the *picker* path `FUN_14081FF70` from `FUN_140769FE0`) and issues the clip at once. Two clip families, two paths: the cutover must keep both straight (see `pes-re-trap-pick.md` §1, "two clip-selection paths").
